=== NFR-3 Browser support

The system shall support modern evergreen browsers on desktop and mobile platforms.

.Acceptance criteria
. Chrome 90+ (desktop and mobile)
. Firefox 88+ (desktop and mobile)
. Safari 14+ (desktop and mobile)
. Edge 90+ (desktop)
. Responsive design works on mobile (320px+), tablet (768px+), desktop (1024px+)
. Progressive Web App (PWA) installable on supported browsers
. Graceful feature degradation if APIs unavailable (camera, geolocation, MediaRecorder)
