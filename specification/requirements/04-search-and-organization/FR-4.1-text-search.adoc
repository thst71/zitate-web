[[FR-4.1]]
=== FR-4.1 Text search

The system shall provide a search text field on home screen that filters entries by text content matching (case-insensitive, partial match).

.Acceptance criteria
. Search text field visible on home screen
. Entering text filters/navigates to search results view
. Results update as user types (debounced 300ms)
. Matches entry text, author name, and labels
. Search highlights matching terms in results
