[[FR-2.1]]
=== FR-2.1 Author assignment

The system shall allow optional assignment of an author to each entry, with author selection from existing authors or creation of new author records.

.Acceptance criteria
. Author field is optional on entry creation
. Dropdown/select shows existing authors (alphabetically sorted)
. "Create New Author" option available
. No duplicate author names allowed (case-insensitive comparison)
