=== US-6.1 Text Search

Effort:: 6 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-4.1>>, <<FR-4.1>>

As a user, I want to search my entries, so that I can find specific quotes.

.Acceptance criteria
. Search box on home screen
. Debounced search (300ms)
. Case-insensitive partial match
. Searches text, author, labels
. Results displayed as cards

.Technical tasks
. Create components/search/SearchBar.tsx
. Implement debounced search hook
. Query IndexedDB with text filter
. Display results in EntryList
. Highlight matching terms (optional)

.Test criteria
* Not recorded before the migration; the tests named in the technical tasks apply.
