=== US-4.3 Audio Recording

Effort:: 8 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-2.6>>, <<TS-3.2>>, <<FR-1.4>>

As a user, I want to record audio for my entry, so that I can capture the spoken quote.

.Acceptance criteria
. Record button starts MediaRecorder
. Timer shows recording duration
. Stop button saves recording
. Max 5 minutes duration
. Audio stored as Blob in IndexedDB
. Playback available after recording

.Technical tasks
. Create hooks/useMediaRecorder.ts
. Implement startRecording with MediaRecorder API
. Implement stopRecording
. Add timer display
. Enforce 5 minute limit
. Save audio blob to IndexedDB
. Add audio player for playback
. Handle browser compatibility (feature detection)

.Test criteria
* Not recorded before the migration; the tests named in the technical tasks apply.
