[[US-2.4]]
=== US-2.4 Entry Save to IndexedDB

Effort:: 4 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-2.2>>, <<TS-2.8>>, <<FR-1.5>>

As a user, I want to save my completed entry, so that it's stored in my collection.

.Acceptance criteria
. Save button creates entry in IndexedDB
. Entry appears immediately in lists/searches
. Form resets after save
. Modal closes or navigates away
. Error handling if save fails

.Technical tasks
. Create hooks/useEntries.ts hook
. Implement addEntry function
. Generate UUID for new entry
. Store entry with timestamp
. Call DBService.add('entries', entry)
. Handle errors (quota exceeded, etc.)
. Reset form on success
. Close modal/navigate
. Write integration tests

.Test criteria
* Not recorded before the migration; the tests named in the technical tasks apply.
