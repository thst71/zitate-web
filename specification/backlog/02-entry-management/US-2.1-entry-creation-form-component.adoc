=== US-2.1 Entry Creation Form Component

Effort:: 6 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-1.2>>, <<FR-1.5>>

As a user, I want to click a plus button to create a new entry, so that I can add a new quote to my collection.

.Acceptance criteria
. Plus button visible on home screen (header or FAB)
. Clicking opens modal or navigates to entry form
. Form includes: text area, location display
. Text field is required
. Cancel and Save buttons

.Technical tasks
. Create components/entry/EntryForm.tsx
. Create components/common/Modal.tsx (if modal approach)
. Add form state management (useState or form library)
. Add plus button to Header component
. Implement open/close modal logic
. Style form (mobile responsive)
. Add keyboard navigation (Escape to close)

.Test criteria
* Not recorded before the migration; the tests named in the technical tasks apply.
