=== US-2.9 Persist Citation Date and URLs

Effort:: 5 h +
Traces to:: <<TS-2.2>>, <<FR-1.7>>, <<FR-1.8>>

As a user, I want to have citation dates and attached URLs saved with each entry, so that this metadata is available across sessions and later edits.

.Acceptance criteria
. IndexedDB entry records include `citationDate` and attached URLs
. Existing add/update entry flows persist the new fields
. Existing entry loading returns citation date and URLs correctly
. Schema migration or backward-compatible defaults handle older entries
. Older entries without the new fields remain readable

.Technical tasks
. Extend IndexedDB `entries` schema with backward-compatible handling
. Update `createEntry` defaults
. Update `useEntries` serialization/deserialization
. Add fallback logic for legacy entries missing the new fields
. Add tests for persistence and backward compatibility

.Test criteria
* Not recorded before the migration; the tests named in the technical tasks apply.
