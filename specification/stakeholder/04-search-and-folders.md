# §4 Search and folders

1. I want to find quotes by typing a part of their text, their author or a label, with results
   while I type.
2. I want folders that collect entries by rules (labels, author, date range, place and radius,
   text) and stay up to date by themselves.
3. I want to create, change, delete and order these folders; deleting a folder keeps its
   entries.
