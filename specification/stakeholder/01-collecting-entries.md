# §1 Collecting entries

1. I want to write down a quote of any length up to a long passage (10,000 characters); an
   empty entry makes no sense.
2. I want to know where I heard or read a quote. The app takes the location from the browser
   when I create an entry, and I can correct it on a map, then or later. Without a location
   permission the app still works.
3. I want to keep up to 10 pictures with an entry, from the camera or from my files, and remove
   single pictures again. Pictures must not fill up the browser storage.
4. I want to record up to 5 minutes of audio with an entry, play it back and remove it.
5. Creating an entry starts with one obvious "plus" button and offers all fields of an entry;
   only the text is required.
6. I want to delete an entry for good, but only after confirming it, together with its pictures
   and audio.
7. I want to keep one or more web links with an entry (source, reference) and see when I added
   each link.
8. The date of a quote (when it was said or published) is not the date I collected it. It starts
   as the creation date, and I can change it at any time.
