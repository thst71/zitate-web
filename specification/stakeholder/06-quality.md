# §6 Quality

1. The home screen with a thousand entries opens within 2 seconds on a desktop and 4 seconds on a
   phone.
2. Nothing I collected gets lost: data survives reloads and crashes, and I can export and
   restore it.
3. The app runs in current Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge on desktop and mobile and can be
   installed as an app.
4. The app asks before using location, camera or microphone, works when I refuse, sends my data
   nowhere and does not track me.
5. I can take my complete collection, media included, to another browser or device by export
   and import.
6. Once loaded, the app works without a network connection.
