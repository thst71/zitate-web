# §5 User interface

1. The app works on phones, tablets and desktops, in light and dark mode, with keyboard, mouse,
   touch and screen reader (WCAG 2.1 AA).
2. The home screen shows a quote from near where I am (or the latest one), the search field, my
   folders and the way to my authors.
3. Search results are compact cards with the beginning of the text, the author and a picture.
4. A folder shows its entries the same way as search results.
5. When there is nothing to show yet, the app tells me so and shows what to do next.
6. Links of an entry are at hand on its card without cluttering the quote text; each shows the
   icon of its site.
