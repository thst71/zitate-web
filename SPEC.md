# Zitate Web – Specification

A personal collection of sayings, citations and "bon mots": captured quickly on any device, with
the place, pictures and sound of the moment, attributed to their authors, labelled and found
again through search and smart folders. The collection lives in the user's browser only.

The sections live in separate files; `SPEC.md §n` refers to the file numbered `n`, `SPEC.md §n.m`
to need `m` in it. Derived from the requirements specification of version 1.x; the requirements
(`specification-web.adoc`) trace back to these needs.
